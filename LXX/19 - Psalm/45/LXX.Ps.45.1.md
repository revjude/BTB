**LXX Psalm 45:1** 

***The Beauty of the Warrior***

To the director; for the changings; to the sons of Korah; for contemplation; an ode for the beloved. 

<small><sub>**LXX:**</sub></small>
My heart discharged forth a good word. I speak  of my works to the king.  My tongue is a reed \[pen\] of a scribe writing fast.
