<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Rehoboth</span> was a city associated with Saul, an Edomite king \([[Gen.36.37|Gen. 36:37]]; [[1Chr.1.48|1 Ch. 1:48]]\).
- A location near Edom Zered **<big>[[H2218|זֶרֶד]]</big>** first mentioned at [[Gen.36.37|Gen.36.37]]; 
- only referred to as Rehoboth **<big>[[H7344|רְחֹבוֹת]]</big>**.<br>

Rehoboth is mentioned as the hometown of Saul, a king of Edom. He is described as "Saul from Rehoboth on the river" \([[Gen.36.37|Gen. 36:37]]; [[1Chr.1.48|1 Ch. 1:48]]\). This distinguishes it from Isaac's well called Rehoboth. It was likely located along the Euphrates River, but its exact location is unknown.
