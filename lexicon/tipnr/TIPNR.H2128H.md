Ziph was a town in the hill country of Judah, southeast of Hebron \([[Josh.15.55|Jos.15:55]]\). The wilderness near Ziph was where David hid from King Saul \([[1Sam.23.14|1Sa.23:14-15]]\). On two occasions, the Ziphites informed Saul of David's whereabouts \([[1Sam.23.19|1Sa.23:19]]; [[1Sam.26.1|26:1]]\). In the wilderness of Ziph, David spared Saul's life when he had the opportunity to kill him \([[1Sam.26.2|1Sa.26:2-12]]\). The town of Ziph is probably to be identified with Tell Zif, about 4 miles southeast of Hebron. Ruins have been found there, along with a spring. [[Ps.54|Psalm 54]] is associated with David's time hiding from Saul in the region of Ziph.
