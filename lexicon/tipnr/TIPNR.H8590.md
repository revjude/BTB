Taanach, also known as Aner, was a Canaanite city located in the territory allotted to the tribe of Manasseh \([[Josh.12.21|Josh. 12:21]]; [[Josh.17.11|17:11]]\). However, the Manassites did not drive out the Canaanites from Taanach, and instead put them to forced labor \([[Judg.1.27|Judg. 1:27]]\). Taanach was later designated as a Levitical city given to the Kohathite clan \([[Josh.21.25|Josh. 21:25]]; [[1Chr.6.70|1 Chr. 6:70]]\). The city is mentioned in the song of Deborah as the site of a battle against the Canaanite king Jabin and his commander Sisera \([[Judg.5.19|Judg. 5:19]]\). It was also included in the administrative district overseen by Baana, one of Solomon's twelve officers \([[1Kgs.4.12|1 Kgs. 4:12]]\). The strategic location of Taanach, near the intersection of important trade routes, made it a significant city in the region.
