<small>*Male • Returned priest • Head of priestly family of Malluchi*</small>

Man living at the time of Exile and Return

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Jonathan</span> was the head of the priestly family of Malluchi during the high priesthood of Joiakim.
- A priest living at the time of Exile and Return, only mentioned at [[Neh.12.14|Neh.12.14]]; 
- only referred to as Jonathan **<big>[[H3129|יוֹנָתָן]]</big>**.

In [[Neh.12.14|Nehemiah 12:14]], Jonathan is listed as the head of the priestly family of Malluchi \(or Malluch\) during the days of Joiakim the high priest. This was in the post-exilic period after the return from Babylon, likely in the late 5th century BC. The priests were organized into groupings called families, with a head over each family. [[Neh.12|Nehemiah 12]] records the heads of priestly families in the generations after the first exiles returned under Jeshua the high priest \([[Neh.12.1|Nehemiah 12:1-7]]\) and then in the time of the high priest Joiakim \([[Neh.12.12|Nehemiah 12:12-21]]\).
