Noah was the son of Lamech, a descendant of Seth \([[Gen.5.29|Genesis 5:29-30]]\). He was a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time, and he walked faithfully with God \([[Gen.6.9|Genesis 6:9]]\). When God saw the wickedness of humanity, He chose Noah to build an ark to save his family and representatives of all animals from the coming flood \([[Gen.6.13|Genesis 6:13-22]]\). Noah obeyed God and built the ark, and he and his family entered it along with the animals \([[Gen.7|Genesis 7]]\). After the flood, Noah built an altar and made sacrifices to God \([[Gen.8.20|Genesis 8:20]]\). God blessed Noah and his sons, establishing a covenant with them and setting the rainbow as a sign of His promise never to destroy the earth by flood again \([[Gen.9|Genesis 9]]\). Noah lived for 950 years \([[Gen.9.29|Genesis 9:29]]\). He is remembered as an example of faith \([[Heb.11.7|Hebrews 11:7]]\) and righteousness \([[Ezek.14.14|Ezekiel 14:14]], [[Ezek.14.20|20]]; [[2Pet.2.5|2 Peter 2:5]]\).
