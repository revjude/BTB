<small>*Male • Returned priest • Head of the priestly family of Shebaniah*</small>

Man living at the time of Exile and Return

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Joseph</span> was the head of the priestly family of Shebaniah during the high priesthood of Joiakim.
- A priest living at the time of Exile and Return, only mentioned at [[Neh.12.14|Neh.12.14]]; 
- only referred to as Joseph **<big>[[H3130|יוֹסֵף]]</big>**.

Joseph is mentioned in [[Neh.12.14|Nehemiah 12:14]] as the head of the priestly family of Shebaniah during the days of Joiakim, the high priest. This was during the post-exilic period in Jerusalem, likely in the 5th century BC. The priests were organized into divisions or families, each with a designated leader. [[Neh.12|Nehemiah 12]] lists the heads of priestly families in the generation following the return from Babylonian exile under Jeshua \([[Neh.12.1|Nehemiah 12:1-7]]\) and then during the time of Joiakim \([[Neh.12.12|Nehemiah 12:12-21]]\).
