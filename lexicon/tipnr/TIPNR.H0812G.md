- only referred to as Eshcol **<big>[[H0812|אֶשְׁכֹּל]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Mamre **<big>[[H4471|מַמְרֵא]]</big>** and Aner **<big>[[H6063|עָנֵר]]</big>**.

Eshcol was an Amorite and one of the three allies of Abram \(later called Abraham\) mentioned in [[Gen.14.13|Genesis 14:13]],[[Gen.14.24|24]]. He, along with Mamre and Aner, joined forces with Abram to rescue his nephew Lot, who had been taken captive by King Chedorlaomer and his allies \([[Gen.14.14|Genesis 14:14-16]]\). The valley of Eshcol, known for its abundant grape clusters, was named after him \([[Num.13.23|Numbers 13:23-24]]\). This valley was later part of the land allocated to the tribe of Judah \([[Num.13.23|Numbers 13:23]]; [[Num.32.9|32:9]]\).
