Amram was a Levite, the son of Kohath and father of Moses, Aaron, and Miriam \([[Exod.6.18|Exo.6.18]],[[Exod.6.20|20]]; [[Num.26.58|Num.26.58]],[[Num.26.59|59]]; [[1Chr.6.2|1Ch.6.2]],[[1Chr.6.3|3]]; [[1Chr.23.12|23.12]],[[1Chr.23.13|13]]\). He married his father's sister Jochebed \([[Exod.6.20|Exo.6.20]]\). Amram's family belonged to the Kohathite branch of Levites \([[Num.3.19|Num.3.19]],[[Num.3.27|27]]; [[1Chr.6.18|1Ch.6.18]]; [[1Chr.24.20|24.20]]; [[1Chr.26.23|26.23]]\). As the father of Moses and Aaron, Amram played a significant role in the history of Israel, although little is recorded about his own life.
