Maacah \(or Maacath\) is the name of a region near Geshur, east of the Jordan River \([[Deut.3.14|Deuteronomy 3:14]]; [[Josh.12.5|Joshua 12:5]]; [[Josh.13.11|13:11]], [[Josh.13.13|13]]\). The Israelites allowed the Geshurites and Maacathites to remain in the land \([[Josh.13.13|Joshua 13:13]]\). Maacah is also mentioned as one of the regions from which the Ammonites hired chariots and horsemen to fight against David \([[2Sam.10.6|2 Samuel 10:6]], [[2Sam.10.8|8]]; [[1Chr.19.7|1 Chronicles 19:7]]\).
