Naaran, also known as Naarah, was a town on the border between the tribal territories of Ephraim and Benjamin \([[Josh.16.7|Josh. 16:7]]; [[1Chr.7.28|1 Chr. 7:28]]\). It was located east of Bethel and near Jericho. Naaran is mentioned as one of the cities on the eastern border of Ephraim, along with Gezer, Michmethath, and Taanath-shiloh.
