Baasha was the third king of the northern kingdom of Israel, ruling for 24 years \([[1Kgs.15.33|1 Kings 15:33]]\). He came to power by assassinating Nadab, the son of Jeroboam, and then proceeded to kill off all of Jeroboam's family \([[1Kgs.15.27|1 Kings 15:27-29]]\). Despite this, Baasha continued in the sinful ways of Jeroboam \([[1Kgs.15.34|1 Kings 15:34]]\). He engaged in warfare with Asa, king of Judah, who bribed Ben-hadad of Syria to attack Israel \([[1Kgs.15.16|1 Kings 15:16-22]]; [[2Chr.16.1|2 Chronicles 16:1-6]]\). The prophet Jehu pronounced judgment on Baasha's dynasty, predicting its downfall \([[1Kgs.16.1|1 Kings 16:1-4]]\). After Baasha's death, his son Elah became king \([[1Kgs.16.6|1 Kings 16:6]]\). Years later, Baasha's sins were remembered alongside those of other wicked kings \([[1Kgs.21.22|1 Kings 21:22]]; [[2Kgs.9.9|2 Kings 9:9]]\). [[Jer.41.9|Jeremiah 41:9]] mentions a pit made by Baasha during his war with Asa, which was later used to dispose of bodies.
