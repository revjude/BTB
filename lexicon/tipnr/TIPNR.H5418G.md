Nethaniah was the father of Ishmael, a man of royal blood who assassinated Gedaliah, the governor appointed by the Babylonians over Judah after the fall of Jerusalem \([[2Kgs.25.23|2 Kings 25:23]],[[2Kgs.25.25|25]]; [[Jer.40.8|Jeremiah 40:8]],[[Jer.40.14|14]],[[Jer.40.15|15]]; [[Jer.41.1|41:1-18]]\). Ishmael's mother was the daughter of Elnathan, an official in Jerusalem \([[2Kgs.24.8|2 Kings 24:8]]\). After the murder of Gedaliah, Ishmael fled to the Ammonites. Nethaniah is only mentioned in reference to his son Ishmael.
