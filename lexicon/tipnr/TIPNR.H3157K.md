Man living at the time of Divided Monarchy

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Jezreel</span> was the name given to Hosea's son as a sign of God's judgment on the house of Jehu \([[Hos.1.4|Hos.1.4]]\).
- A man living at the time of Divided Monarchy, only mentioned at [[Hos.1.4|Hos.1.4]]; 
- only referred to as Jezreel **<big>[[H3157|יִזְרְעֵאל]]</big>**; 
-  son of Hosea **<big>[[H1954|הוֹשֵׁעַ]]</big>** and Gomer **<big>[[H1586|גֹּמֶר]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Lo-ruhamah **<big>[[H3819|לֹא רֻחָמָה]]</big>** , Lo-ammi H3818\).

In [[Hos.1.4|Hosea 1:4]], God instructs the prophet Hosea to name his son Jezreel as a symbolic act representing divine judgment. The name Jezreel is associated with the valley of Jezreel, where Jehu, a former king of Israel, had carried out a massacre \([[2Kgs.9|2Ki.9-10]]\). God declares that he will punish the house of Jehu for the bloodshed at Jezreel and bring an end to the kingdom of Israel. The child's name serves as a prophetic sign of the coming judgment and the end of Jehu's dynasty.
