Amos was a prophet from Tekoa who prophesied during the reigns of Uzziah, king of Judah, and Jeroboam, king of Israel \([[Amos.1.1|Amos 1:1]]\). He was originally a shepherd and fig grower before being called by God to deliver His message \([[Amos.7.14|Amos 7:14-15]]\). Amos prophesied against the sins of Israel, warning them of impending judgment \([[Amos.7.8|Amos 7:8]]; [[Amos.8.2|8:2]]\). His prophecies were met with opposition from Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, who told him to stop prophesying and leave \([[Amos.7.10|Amos 7:10-13]]\). However, Amos remained faithful to his calling and continued to proclaim God's word.
