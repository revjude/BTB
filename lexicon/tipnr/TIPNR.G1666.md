Prophet living at the time of Divided Monarchy

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Elisha</span> was a prophet who performed many miracles and served as a spiritual leader in Israel after Elijah \([[1Kgs.19|1Ki.19]]; [[2Kgs.2|2Ki.2-13]]\).

- referred to as Elisha **<big>[[H0477|אֱלִישָׁע]]</big>**, or Elisha **<big>[[G1666|Ἐλισσαῖος]]</big>**;

Elisha was a prophet in Israel who succeeded Elijah \([[1Kgs.19.16|1Ki.19.16]]\). He was the son of Shaphat and was from Abel-meholah \([[1Kgs.19.16|1Ki.19.16]],[[1Kgs.19.19|19]]\). Elisha became Elijah's servant and disciple after Elijah threw his cloak around him while he was plowing \([[1Kgs.19.19|1Ki.19.19]]\). When Elijah was taken up to heaven in a whirlwind, Elisha took up his cloak and received a double portion of his spirit \([[2Kgs.2.9|2Ki.2.9-13]]\). Elisha performed many miracles, including parting the Jordan River \([[2Kgs.2.14|2Ki.2.14]]\), purifying water \([[2Kgs.2.19|2Ki.2.19-22]]\), multiplying a widow's oil \([[2Kgs.4.1|2Ki.4.1-7]]\), raising a Shunammite woman's son from the dead \([[2Kgs.4.8|2Ki.4.8-37]]\), and healing Naaman's leprosy \([[2Kgs.5|2Ki.5]]\). He also prophesied and gave counsel to kings, such as Jehoshaphat and Joram \([[2Kgs.3|2Ki.3]]\). Elisha's ministry spanned the reigns of several kings of Israel, including Joram, Jehu, Jehoahaz, and Joash \([[2Kgs.2|2Ki.2-13]]\). He died during the reign of King Joash \([[2Kgs.13.14|2Ki.13.14-20]]\). Jesus mentioned Elisha as an example of a prophet who was sent to heal a foreigner, Naaman the Syrian \([[Luke.4.27|Luk.4.27]]\).
