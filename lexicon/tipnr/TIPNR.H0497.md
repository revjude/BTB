Man living at the time of Divided Monarchy

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Eleadah</span> was a son of Ephraim and an ancestor of Joshua.
- A man from the tribe of Ephraim living at the time of Divided Monarchy, only mentioned at [[1Chr.7.20|1Ch.7.20]]; 
- only referred to as Eleadah **<big>[[H0497|אֶלְעָדָה]]</big>**; 
- a son of Tahath **<big>[[H8480|תַּחַת]]</big>**; 
-  father of Tahath **<big>[[H8480|תַּחַת]]</big>**.

Eleadah is briefly mentioned in [[1Chr.7.20|1 Chronicles 7:20]] as a son of Ephraim, the son of Joseph. He is listed among the descendants of Ephraim, who was one of the twelve tribes of Israel. Eleadah is significant as an ancestor of Joshua, the prominent leader who succeeded Moses and led the Israelites into the Promised Land.
