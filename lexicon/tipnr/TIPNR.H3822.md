<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Lebaoth</span>, a town in the Negev region of Judah \([[Josh.15.32|Joshua 15:32]]\).
- A location near Madmannah **<big>[[H4089|מַדְמַנָּה]]</big>** only mentioned at [[Josh.15.32|Jos.15.32]]; 
- only referred to as Lebaoth **<big>[[H3822|לְבָאוֹת]]</big>**.<br>

Lebaoth is mentioned in [[Josh.15.32|Joshua 15:32]] as one of the towns in the Negev region, the southern part of the territory allocated to the tribe of Judah. It is listed along with other towns such as Shilhim, Ain, and Rimmon. Its exact location is not specified.
