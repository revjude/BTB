<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Abdi</span>, a Levite, was an ancestor of Ethan, one of the musicians appointed by David to serve in the temple.
- A man from the tribe of Levi living at the time of Divided Monarchy, only mentioned at [[1Chr.6.44|1Ch.6.44]]; 
- only referred to as Abdi **<big>[[H5660|עַבְדִּי]]</big>**; 
- a son of Malluch **<big>[[H4409|מַלּוּךְ]]</big>**; 
-  father of Kishi **<big>[[H7029|קִישִׁי]]</big>**.

Abdi is mentioned in [[1Chr.6.44|1 Chronicles 6:44]] as a Levite and an ancestor of Ethan, one of the musicians appointed by King David to serve in the temple. Ethan, along with Heman and Asaph, was a leading musician and singer during David's reign \([[1Chr.15.19|1 Chronicles 15:19]]\). Abdi is listed in the genealogy of the Levites, specifically within the lineage of Merari, one of the three main Levitical clans.
