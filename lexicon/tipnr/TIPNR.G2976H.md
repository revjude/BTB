Mary's brother living at the time of the New Testament

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Lazarus</span>, the brother of Mary and Martha, was raised from the dead by Jesus \([[John.11.1|Jn.11.1-44]]; [[John.12.1|12.1-2]],[[John.12.9|9-11]]\).
- Mary's brother living at the time of the New Testament, first mentioned at [[John.11.1|Jhn.11.1]]; 
- only referred to as Lazarus **<big>[[G2976|Λάζαρος]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Martha **<big>[[G3136|Μάρθα]]</big>** and Mary **<big>[[G3137|Μαρία]]</big>**.

Lazarus was the brother of Mary and Martha, who lived in the village of Bethany \([[John.11.1|Jn.11.1]]\). Jesus loved Lazarus and his sisters \([[John.11.5|Jn.11.5]]\). When Lazarus fell ill, his sisters sent word to Jesus, but by the time Jesus arrived, Lazarus had been dead for four days \([[John.11.6|Jn.11.6]],[[John.11.17|17]]\). Jesus went to the tomb and called for Lazarus to come out, and Lazarus emerged, still wrapped in burial cloths \([[John.11.43|Jn.11.43-44]]\). This miraculous sign led many to believe in Jesus \([[John.11.45|Jn.11.45]]\). Later, Jesus visited Lazarus' home, where Martha served a meal and Mary anointed Jesus' feet with perfume \([[John.12.1|Jn.12.1-3]]\). The chief priests plotted to kill Lazarus because his resurrection caused many to believe in Jesus \([[John.12.9|Jn.12.9-11]]\). Lazarus' story demonstrates Jesus' power over death and foreshadows His own resurrection.
