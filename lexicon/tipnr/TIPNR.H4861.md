Mishal, also known as Mashal, was a city allotted to the tribe of Asher \([[Josh.19.26|Joshua 19:26]]\). It was later designated as a Levitical city, given to the Gershonites \([[Josh.21.30|Joshua 21:30]]; [[1Chr.6.74|1 Chronicles 6:74]]\). The Levites, who were not allotted a specific territory, were given cities throughout Israel to dwell in, along with pasture lands for their livestock \([[Num.35.1|Numbers 35:1-8]]\). No further details about the location are provided in the Bible.
