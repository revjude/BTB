Man living at the time of Divided Monarchy

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Haroeh</span> or Reaiah was the father of half the inhabitants of Manuhoth. \([[1Chr.2.52|1Ch.2.52]]; [[1Chr.4.2|4.2]]\)
- A man from the tribe of Judah living at the time of Divided Monarchy, first mentioned at [[1Chr.2.52|1Ch.2.52]]; 
- referred to as Haroeh **<big>[[H7204|רֹאֵה]]</big>**, or Reaiah **<big>[[H7211|רְאָיָה]]</big>**; 
- a son of Shobal **<big>[[H7732|שׁוֹבָל]]</big>**; 
-  father of Jahath **<big>[[H3189|יַחַת]]</big>**.

Haroeh, also known as Reaiah, was the father of half the inhabitants of Manuhoth. Manuhoth was likely a town or village in Judah. Haroeh is mentioned in the genealogies of Judah recorded in the book of 1 Chronicles. He was a descendant of Shobal, who was a descendant of Judah's son Hur. No other information is provided about Haroeh or Reaiah in the Bible. \([[1Chr.2.52|1Ch.2.52]]; [[1Chr.4.2|4.2]]\).
