<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Iddo</span> \(or Adaiah\), a Levite in the genealogy of the temple singers.
- A man from the tribe of Levi living at the time of Divided Monarchy, first mentioned at [[1Chr.6.21|1Ch.6.21]]; 
- referred to as Iddo **<big>[[H5714|עִדּוֹ]]</big>**, or Adaiah **<big>[[H5718|עֲדָיָה]]</big>**; 
- a son of Joah **<big>[[H3098|יוֹאָח]]</big>**; 
-  father of Zerah **<big>[[H2226|זֶרַח]]</big>**.

Iddo, also known as Adaiah, appears in the genealogy of the Levites who served as temple singers \([[1Chr.6.21|1 Chronicles 6:21]],[[1Chr.6.41|41]]\). He is listed as a descendant of Gershom, one of the sons of Levi. The genealogy traces the lineage of the temple singers from the time of King David to the post-exilic period. Iddo's mention in this context suggests that he was a Levite who played a role in the musical ministry of the temple.
