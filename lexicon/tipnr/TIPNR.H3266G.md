Edomite living at the time of the Patriarchs

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Jeush</span>, a son of Esau and Oholibamah, was a chief of Edom \([[Gen.36.5|Gen.36.5]],[[Gen.36.14|14]],[[Gen.36.18|18]]; [[1Chr.1.35|1Ch.1.35]]\).
- A man of the Edomites living at the time of the Patriarchs, first mentioned at [[Gen.36.5|Gen.36.5]]; 
- referred to as Jeush **<big>[[H3266|יְעוּשׁ]]</big>**, or Jeush \(=Qere. Ketiv= Jeish\)  **<big>[[H3274|יְעִישׁ]]</big>**; 
-  son of Esau **<big>[[H6215|עֵשָׂו]]</big>** and Oholibamah **<big>[[H0173|אׇהֳלִיבָמָה]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Jalam **<big>[[H3281|יַעְלָם]]</big>** and Korah **<big>[[H7141|קֹרַח]]</big>**; 
-  half-brother of: Eliphaz **<big>[[H0464|אֱלִיפַז]]</big>** and Reuel **<big>[[H7467|רְעוּאֵל]]</big>**.

Jeush was one of the sons of Esau, the twin brother of Israel \(or 'Jacob'\), and Esau's wife Oholibamah, as recorded in [[Gen.36.5|Genesis 36:5]],[[Gen.36.14|14]] and [[1Chr.1.35|1 Chronicles 1:35]]. He is listed among the chiefs of Edom who descended from Esau. As a grandson of Isaac and great-grandson of Abraham, Jeush was part of the Edomite clan that settled in the region of Seir.
