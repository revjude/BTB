Jezreel was a prominent city in the Jezreel Valley, located in the territory of Issachar \([[Josh.19.18|Joshua 19:18]]\). It served as one of the royal residences of King Ahab and Queen Jezebel \([[1Kgs.21.1|1 Kings 21:1]]\). The city is best known for its association with the vineyard of Naboth, which Ahab coveted and acquired through Jezebel's scheming, leading to the prophecy of their downfall \([[1Kgs.21|1 Kings 21]]\). Jezreel was also the scene of Jehu's coup against the house of Ahab, where he killed Joram \(Ahab's son\) and Jezebel \([[2Kgs.9|2 Kings 9]]\). The name "Jezreel" is used symbolically in the Book of Hosea to represent God's judgment on the house of Jehu for the bloodshed at Jezreel \([[Hos.1.4|Hosea 1:4]]\) and the future restoration of Israel \([[Hos.1.11|Hosea 1:11]]\). The city was a significant administrative center during the reigns of the kings of Israel and was known for its strategic location and fertile surroundings.
