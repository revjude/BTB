<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Oreb</span> was a Midianite prince who was killed by the Ephraimites during the time of Gideon.

- only referred to as Oreb **<big>[[H6159|עֹרֵב]]</big>**.

Oreb was a prince of Midian who, along with another prince named Zeeb, led the Midianite army against Israel during the time of Gideon \([[Judg.7.25|Judges 7:25]]\). After Gideon and his 300 men defeated the Midianites, the Ephraimites captured and killed Oreb and Zeeb \([[Judg.7.25|Judges 7:25]]; [[Judg.8.3|8:3]]\). The Ephraimites brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon as a trophy of their victory \([[Judg.7.25|Judges 7:25]]\). The rock where Oreb was killed became known as the "rock of Oreb" \([[Judg.7.25|Judges 7:25]]\). The defeat of Oreb and Zeeb was a significant victory for Israel and a testament to God's power and deliverance \([[Ps.83.11|Psalm 83:11]]\). The prophet Isaiah later used the "rock of Oreb" as a symbol of God's judgment against the Assyrians \([[Isa.10.26|Isaiah 10:26]]\).
