Moza was a descendant of King Saul and part of the tribe of Benjamin. He was the son of Zimri and father of Binea \([[1Chr.8.36|1 Chronicles 8:36-37]], [[1Chr.9.42|9:42-43]]\). These genealogies trace Saul's lineage after his death. No other information is provided about Moza's life or achievements. He should not be confused with Moza, the son of Caleb \([[1Chr.2.46|1 Chronicles 2:46]]\).
