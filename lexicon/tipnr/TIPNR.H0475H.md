Man living at the time of Divided Monarchy

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Eliashib</span> was a priest who served during the reign of King David.
- A man living at the time of Divided Monarchy, only mentioned at [[1Chr.24.12|1Ch.24.12]]; 
- only referred to as Eliashib **<big>[[H0475|אֶלְיָשִׁיב]]</big>**.

Eliashib is mentioned briefly in [[1Chr.24.12|1 Chronicles 24:12]] as the head of the eleventh priestly division organized by King David. David divided the priests into twenty-four divisions to ensure a systematic and orderly service in the temple. Each division was responsible for serving in the temple for a week at a time, twice a year. As the head of the eleventh division, Eliashib would have been responsible for overseeing his division's duties and ensuring that they fulfilled their responsibilities in the temple service.
