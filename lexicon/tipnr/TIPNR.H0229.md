Ezbai is mentioned only once in the Bible, in [[1Chr.11.37|1 Chronicles 11:37]]. He was the father of Naarai, who was one of King David's mighty men. The mighty men were a group of elite warriors who played a significant role in David's military campaigns and in establishing his kingdom \([[2Sam.23.8|2 Samuel 23:8-39]]; [[1Chr.11.10|1 Chronicles 11:10-47]]\). Naarai, son of Ezbai, is listed among these mighty men, indicating that he was a skilled and valiant warrior.
