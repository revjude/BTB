Hebron was a son of Kohath and a grandson of Levi. He is mentioned in the genealogies of the Levites in Exodus, Numbers, and 1 Chronicles. Hebron's brothers were Amram, Izhar, and Uzziel \([[Exod.6.18|Exo.6.18]]; [[Num.3.19|Num.3.19]]; [[1Chr.6.2|1Ch.6.2]],[[1Chr.6.18|18]]\). His descendants were known as the Hebronites, who were assigned various duties in the service of the tabernacle and the temple \([[Num.3.27|Num.3.27]]; [[Num.26.58|26.58]]; [[1Chr.26.23|1Ch.26.23]],[[1Chr.26.30|30]],[[1Chr.26.31|31]]\). Some of the notable Hebronites included Jeriah, the chief of the Hebronites during the time of David \([[1Chr.23.19|1Ch.23.19]]; [[1Chr.26.31|26.31]]\), and Hashabiah, a leader during the time of David \([[1Chr.15.9|1Ch.15.9]]\).
