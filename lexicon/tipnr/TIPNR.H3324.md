Izhar, also known as Amminadab, was a Levite, the son of Kohath and grandson of Levi \([[Exod.6.18|Exo. 6:18]], [[Exod.6.21|21]]; [[Num.3.19|Num. 3:19]]; [[1Chr.6.2|1Ch. 6:2]], [[1Chr.6.18|18]], [[1Chr.6.38|38]]\). He was the father of Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri \([[Exod.6.21|Exo. 6:21]]; [[1Chr.6.22|1Ch. 6:22]]\). Izhar's descendants, the Izharites, were one of the clans of the Kohathites \([[Num.3.27|Num. 3:27]]; [[1Chr.24.22|1Ch. 24:22]]; [[1Chr.26.23|26:23]], [[1Chr.26.29|29]]\). 

The Kohathites, including the Izharites, were responsible for carrying the sacred objects of the tabernacle during Israel's journey in the wilderness \([[Num.4.15|Num. 4:15]], [[Num.4.18|18-20]]\). They were also assigned duties in the tabernacle and, later, in the temple \([[1Chr.6.33|1Ch. 6:33-38]]; [[1Chr.23.12|23:12-20]]; [[1Chr.24.20|24:20-31]]\). Some of the Izharites served as officials and judges during King David's reign \([[1Chr.26.29|1Ch. 26:29-30]]\). 

Izhar's most notable descendant was Korah, who led a rebellion against Moses and Aaron \([[Num.16.1|Num. 16:1]]\). Despite Korah's rebellion, the Izharites continued to serve as Levites in Israel.
