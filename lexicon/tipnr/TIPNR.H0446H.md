<small>*Male • Son of Pallu • Reubenite, father of Dathan and Abiram*</small>

Man living at the time of Egypt and Wilderness

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Eliab</span>, a Reubenite, was the father of Dathan and Abiram, who rebelled against Moses.
- A man from the tribe of Reuben living at the time of Egypt and Wilderness, first mentioned at [[Num.16.1|Num.16.1]]; 
- only referred to as Eliab **<big>[[H0446|אֱלִיאָב]]</big>**; 
- a son of Pallu **<big>[[H6396|פַּלּוּא]]</big>**; 
-  father of Dathan **<big>[[H1885|דָּתָן]]</big>**, Abiram **<big>[[H0048|אֲבִירָם]]</big>** and Nemuel **<big>[[H5241|נְמוּאֵל]]</big>**.

Eliab, a member of the tribe of Reuben, is mentioned in [[Num.16.1|Numbers 16:1]],[[Num.16.12|12]]; [[Num.26.8|26:8]],[[Num.26.9|9]]; and [[Deut.11.6|Deuteronomy 11:6]] as the father of Dathan and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram, along with Korah, led a rebellion against the leadership of Moses and Aaron in the wilderness. They challenged the authority of Moses and Aaron, claiming that all the congregation was holy \([[Num.16.1|Numbers 16:1-3]]\). Moses summoned Dathan and Abiram, but they refused to appear before him \([[Num.16.12|Numbers 16:12-14]]\). As a result, God caused the earth to open up and swallow the rebels and their households \([[Num.16.31|Numbers 16:31-33]]\). Eliab's sons, Dathan and Abiram, perished in this divine judgment \([[Num.26.9|Numbers 26:9]]; [[Deut.11.6|Deuteronomy 11:6]]\). The rebellion of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram serves as a warning against challenging God's appointed leaders.
