Man living at the time of Exile and Return

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Hanan</span> was among the temple servants who returned to Jerusalem from Babylonian captivity.
- A man living at the time of Exile and Return, first mentioned at [[Ezra.2.46|Ezr.2.46]]; 
- only referred to as Hanan **<big>[[H2605|חָנָן]]</big>**.

Hanan is mentioned in [[Ezra.2.46|Ezra 2:46]] and [[Neh.7.49|Nehemiah 7:49]] as one of the temple servants \(Nethinim\) who returned to Jerusalem from Babylonian captivity. The temple servants were a group of people assigned to assist the Levites in the maintenance and service of the temple. They were not of Israelite descent but were likely descendants of the Gibeonites who had been assigned by Joshua to serve in the tabernacle \([[Josh.9.27|Joshua 9:27]]\). Hanan and the other temple servants are listed among those who returned to help rebuild the temple and restore worship in Jerusalem.
