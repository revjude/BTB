Elimelech was a man from Bethlehem in Judah, the husband of Naomi and the father of Mahlon and Chilion \([[Ruth.1.2|Rut.1.2]]\). During a time of famine, Elimelech and his family moved to the country of Moab \([[Ruth.1.1|Rut.1.1-2]]\). While in Moab, Elimelech died, leaving Naomi a widow \([[Ruth.1.3|Rut.1.3]]\). Naomi's sons, Mahlon and Chilion, married Moabite women named Ruth and Orpah, but they also died after about ten years \([[Ruth.1.4|Rut.1.4-5]]\). After the death of her husband and sons, Naomi decided to return to Bethlehem, and her daughter-in-law Ruth accompanied her \([[Ruth.1.6|Rut.1.6-22]]\). Elimelech's family had owned land in Bethlehem, which was later acquired by Boaz, a relative who married Ruth \([[Ruth.4.3|Rut.4.3]],[[Ruth.4.9|9]]\). Through this marriage, Elimelech's lineage was preserved, and his family's property remained within the clan.
