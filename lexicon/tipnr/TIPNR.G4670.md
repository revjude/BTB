Sodom was an ancient city located in the plain of Jordan, known for its great wickedness \([[Gen.13.13|Gen. 13:13]]\). It was one of the five "cities of the plain" \([[Gen.14.2|Gen. 14:2]]\) and was closely associated with Gomorrah. God determined to destroy Sodom and Gomorrah due to their grave sins \([[Gen.18.20|Gen. 18:20]]\), but Abraham pleaded with God to spare the city if even ten righteous people could be found \([[Gen.18.22|Gen. 18:22-33]]\). However, not even ten were found, and God rained down burning sulfur on Sodom and Gomorrah, completely destroying them \([[Gen.19.24|Gen. 19:24-25]]\). Lot and his daughters were the only survivors, as they were warned by angels to flee \([[Gen.19.15|Gen. 19:15-16]]\). Sodom became a byword for wickedness and divine judgment \([[Deut.29.23|Deut. 29:23]]; [[Isa.1.9|Isa. 1:9-10]]; [[Jer.23.14|Jer. 23:14]]; [[Ezek.16.46|Ezek. 16:46-56]]; [[Matt.10.15|Matt. 10:15]]; [[Matt.11.23|11:23-24]]; [[Luke.17.29|Luke 17:29]]; [[Rom.9.29|Rom. 9:29]]; [[2Pet.2.6|2 Pet. 2:6]]; [[Jude.1.7|Jude 1:7]]; [[Rev.11.8|Rev. 11:8]]\).
