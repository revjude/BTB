Korah was a Levite who led a rebellion against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness \([[Num.16|Num.16]]\). He was the son of Izhar, from the Kohathite clan \([[Exod.6.21|Exo.6.21]],[[Exod.6.24|24]]\). Korah, along with Dathan, Abiram, and 250 well-known community leaders, accused Moses and Aaron of exalting themselves above the congregation \([[Num.16.1|Num.16.1-3]]\). When Korah's followers presented incense offerings, the Lord destroyed them with fire \([[Num.16.35|Num.16.35]]\). The earth opened and swallowed Korah, Dathan, Abiram, and their households \([[Num.16.31|Num.16.31-33]]\). Korah's sons, however, did not die \([[Num.26.11|Num.26.11]]\). The bronze censers of the rebels were made into a covering for the altar, as a reminder not to rebel against the Lord \([[Num.16.38|Num.16.38-40]]\). Korah's descendants became temple musicians \([[1Chr.6.22|1Ch.6.22]],[[1Chr.6.37|37]]; [[Ps.42|Psa.42-49]]; [[Ps.84|84-88]]\) and gatekeepers \([[1Chr.9.19|1Ch.9.19]]; [[1Chr.26.1|26.1]],[[1Chr.26.19|19]]\). Some were mighty warriors who joined David at Ziklag \([[1Chr.12.6|1Ch.12.6]]\), and others settled in Jerusalem after the exile \([[1Chr.9.31|1Ch.9.31]]\). The Korahites also sang at the reign of Jehoshaphat \([[2Chr.20.19|2Ch.20.19]]\) and were listed among the Levite clans \([[Num.26.58|Num.26.58]]\).
