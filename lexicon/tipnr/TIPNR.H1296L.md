Berechiah was the father of the prophet Zechariah, who prophesied during the reign of Darius, king of Persia \([[Zech.1.1|Zec. 1:1]],[[Zech.1.7|7]]\). Zechariah, along with Haggai, encouraged the Jews to rebuild the temple in Jerusalem after their return from Babylonian exile \([[Ezra.5.1|Ezr. 5:1]]; [[Ezra.6.14|6:14]]\). Berechiah is mentioned in the opening verses of the book of Zechariah, establishing the prophet's lineage and the time of his ministry. As the father of a prominent prophet, Berechiah likely played a significant role in his son's spiritual upbringing and education.
