<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Raphah</span> \(or Rephaiah\) was a son of Binea and a descendant of King Saul.
- A man from the tribe of Benjamin living at the time of Divided Monarchy, first mentioned at [[1Chr.8.37|1Ch.8.37]]; 
- referred to as Raphah **<big>[[H7498|רָפָא]]</big>**, or Rephaiah **<big>[[H7509|רְפָיָה]]</big>**; 
-  son of Binea **<big>[[H1150|בִּנְעָא]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Eleasah **<big>[[H0501|אֶלְעָשָׂה]]</big>** and Azel **<big>[[H0682|אָצֵל]]</big>**.

Raphah, also known as Rephaiah, is mentioned in the genealogy of King Saul's descendants \([[1Chr.8.37|1 Chronicles 8:37]]; [[1Chr.9.43|9:43]]\). He was the son of Binea and a member of the tribe of Benjamin. The genealogy traces Saul's lineage after his death, showing that his family line continued even though he was no longer king.
