Nazareth was a village in the region of Galilee, located about 15 miles west of the Sea of Galilee. It is best known as the hometown of Jesus Christ \([[Matt.2.23|Matt. 2:23]]; [[Luke.1.26|Luke 1:26]]; [[Luke.2.39|2:39]], [[Luke.2.51|51]]; [[Luke.4.16|4:16]]\). Jesus was often referred to as "Jesus of Nazareth" or "the Nazarene" \([[Mark.1.24|Mark 1:24]]; [[Mark.10.47|10:47]]; [[Luke.18.37|Luke 18:37]]; [[Luke.24.19|24:19]]; [[John.18.5|John 18:5]], [[John.18.7|7]]; [[John.19.19|19:19]]; [[Acts.2.22|Acts 2:22]]; [[Acts.10.38|10:38]]\). 

Nazareth is not mentioned in the Old Testament or in other ancient literature, suggesting it was a small, relatively insignificant village. The Gospels record that Jesus grew up in Nazareth, preached in its synagogue \([[Luke.4.16|Luke 4:16-30]]\), and was initially rejected by its inhabitants \([[Matt.13.54|Matt. 13:54-58]]; [[Mark.6.1|Mark 6:1-6]]; [[Luke.4.28|Luke 4:28-30]]\). 

In the first century, Nazareth had a negative reputation, as evidenced by Nathanael's question, "Can anything good come out of Nazareth?" \([[John.1.46|John 1:46]]\). However, Jesus' upbringing in Nazareth fulfilled the prophecy that He would be called a Nazarene \([[Matt.2.23|Matt. 2:23]]\), a term possibly derived from the Hebrew word "netzer," meaning "branch" or "shoot," and alluding to the Messiah \([[Isa.11.1|Isa. 11:1]]\).
