Man living at the time of Egypt and Wilderness

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Jamin</span> was a son of Simeon and a grandson of Jacob.
- A man from the tribe of Simeon living at the time of Egypt and Wilderness, first mentioned at [[Gen.46.10|Gen.46.10]]; 
- referred to as Jamin **<big>[[H3226|יָמִין]]</big>**, or Jaminite **<big>[[H3228|יְמִינִי]]</big>**; 
-  son of Simeon **<big>[[H8095|שִׁמְעוֹן]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Jemuel **<big>[[H3223|יְמוּאֵל]]</big>**, Ohad **<big>[[H0161|אֹהַד]]</big>**, Jachin **<big>[[H3199|יָכִין]]</big>**, Zerah **<big>[[H2226|זֶרַח]]</big>** and Shaul **<big>[[H7586|שָׁאוּל]]</big>**.

Jamin was one of the sons of Simeon, the second son of Israel \(or 'Jacob'\) and Leah, as recorded in [[Gen.46.10|Genesis 46:10]], [[Exod.6.15|Exodus 6:15]], and [[1Chr.4.24|1 Chronicles 4:24]]. He was born in Canaan and went down to Egypt with his father and grandfather as part of the seventy members of Jacob's household \([[Gen.46.27|Genesis 46:27]]\). In [[Num.26.12|Numbers 26:12]], Jamin is mentioned as one of the clans of Simeon, suggesting that his descendants formed a significant subgroup within the tribe. The Bible does not provide any further details about Jamin's life or his role in the events of the Israelites' history.
