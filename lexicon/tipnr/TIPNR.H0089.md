- A man living at the time of United Monarchy, only mentioned at [[2Sam.23.11|2Sa.23.11]]; 
- only referred to as Agee **<big>[[H0089|אָגֵא]]</big>**; 
-  father of Shammah **<big>[[H8037|שַׁמָּא]]</big>**.

Agee is mentioned in the Bible as the father of Shammah the Hararite, who was one of King David's mighty men. Shammah is known for his brave stand against the Philistines in a field of lentils, where he defended the field and struck down the Philistines, bringing about a great victory.
