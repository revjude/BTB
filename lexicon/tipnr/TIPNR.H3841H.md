Libnah was a Canaanite city that was conquered by Joshua during the Israelite conquest of the Promised Land \([[Josh.10.29|Joshua 10:29-32]], [[Josh.10.39|39]]; [[Josh.12.15|12:15]]\). It was located in the Shephelah region of Judah \([[Josh.15.42|Joshua 15:42]]\). Libnah was later designated as a Levitical city, given to the descendants of Aaron \([[Josh.21.13|Joshua 21:13]]; [[1Chr.6.57|1 Chronicles 6:57]]\). During the reign of King Joram of Judah, Libnah revolted against Judah's rule \([[2Kgs.8.22|2 Kings 8:22]]; [[2Chr.21.10|2 Chronicles 21:10]]\). The city is also mentioned in the context of King Hezekiah's reign, as the place where the Assyrian commander Rabshakeh returned after speaking with Hezekiah's officials \([[2Kgs.19.8|2 Kings 19:8]]; [[Isa.37.8|Isaiah 37:8]]\). Libnah was the hometown of Hamutal, the mother of King Jehoahaz and King Zedekiah of Judah \([[2Kgs.23.31|2 Kings 23:31]]; [[2Kgs.24.18|24:18]]; [[Jer.52.1|Jeremiah 52:1]]\).
