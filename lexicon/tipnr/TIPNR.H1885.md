<small>*Male • Son of Eliab • Reubenite who rebelled against Moses and Aaron*</small>

Man living at the time of Egypt and Wilderness

<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Dathan</span>, a Reubenite, joined Korah's rebellion against Moses and Aaron and was punished by God. \([[Num.16.1|Num.16.1]],[[Num.16.12|12]],[[Num.16.24|24]],[[Num.16.25|25]],[[Num.16.27|27]],[[Num.16.27|27]]; [[Num.26.9|26.9]],[[Num.26.9|9]]; [[Deut.11.6|Deu.11.6]]; [[Ps.106.17|Psa.106.17]]\)
- A man from the tribe of Reuben living at the time of Egypt and Wilderness, first mentioned at [[Num.16.1|Num.16.1]]; 
- only referred to as Dathan **<big>[[H1885|דָּתָן]]</big>**; 
-  son of Eliab **<big>[[H0446|אֱלִיאָב]]</big>**; 
- a brother of Abiram **<big>[[H0048|אֲבִירָם]]</big>** and Nemuel **<big>[[H5241|נְמוּאֵל]]</big>**.

Dathan, along with his brother Abiram, was a descendant of Reuben who joined Korah, a Levite, in rebelling against the authority of Moses and Aaron \([[Num.16.1|Numbers 16:1]]\). When Moses summoned Dathan and Abiram, they refused to come, accusing Moses of failing to bring them into a land flowing with milk and honey \([[Num.16.12|Numbers 16:12-14]]\). 

As the rebellion escalated, Moses warned the congregation to move away from the tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram \([[Num.16.24|Numbers 16:24-27]]\). God then caused the earth to open up and swallow Dathan, Abiram, and their households, along with Korah and his followers \([[Num.16.31|Numbers 16:31-35]]; [[Ps.106.17|Psalm 106:17]]\). 

The sons of Dathan and Abiram, however, did not die in this judgment \([[Num.26.9|Numbers 26:9-11]]\). The rebellion of Dathan and Abiram is remembered as a warning against defying God's appointed leaders \([[Deut.11.6|Deuteronomy 11:6]]\).
