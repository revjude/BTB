People from Ezrah\(?\)

The <span class='tipnrhighlight'>Ezrahites</span> were descendants of Zerah, known for their wisdom and musical skill, as exemplified by Ethan and Heman.
- People from Ezrah\(?\), first mentioned at [[1Kgs.4.31|1Ki.4.31]]; 
- only referred to as Ezrahites **<big>[[H0250|אֶזְרָחִי]]</big>**.

The Ezrahites were a family or clan within the tribe of Judah, descended from Zerah, one of the sons of Judah \([[Gen.38.30|Genesis 38:30]]; [[1Chr.2.4|1 Chronicles 2:4]]\). They were known for their wisdom and musical abilities. In [[1Kgs.4.31|1 Kings 4:31]], Ethan the Ezrahite is mentioned as one of the wise men whom Solomon surpassed in wisdom. Ethan is also credited with composing [[Ps.89|Psalm 89]] \([[Ps.89.1|Psalm 89:1]]\). Another notable Ezrahite was Heman, who was a singer and musician in the time of David and is mentioned as the author of [[Ps.88|Psalm 88]] \([[Ps.88.1|Psalm 88:1]]; [[1Chr.6.33|1 Chronicles 6:33]]\). The Ezrahites' reputation for wisdom and musical skill suggests that they played a significant role in the cultural and religious life of ancient Israel.
