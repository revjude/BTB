- only referred to as Meshezabel **<big>[[H4898|מְשֵׁיזַבְאֵל]]</big>**.

Meshezabel is mentioned in the book of Nehemiah as one of the leaders who sealed the covenant with Nehemiah \([[Neh.10.21|Neh.10.21]]\). After the completion of the wall of Jerusalem, the people gathered to hear Ezra read the Book of the Law. They then confessed their sins and made a covenant to follow God's laws. Meshezabel was among the leaders who signed and sealed this covenant, pledging to uphold its terms.
