Eleazar, the son of Aaron, is prominently featured in the books of Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, and 1 Chronicles. He was the third son of Aaron and Elisheba \([[Exod.6.23|Exodus 6:23]],[[Exod.6.25|25]]\) and was consecrated as a priest along with his brothers \([[Exod.28.1|Exodus 28:1]]\). After the deaths of his older brothers, Nadab and Abihu \([[Lev.10.1|Leviticus 10:1-2]]\), Eleazar became the chief priest under his father \([[Num.3.32|Numbers 3:32]]\). He assisted Moses in the census \([[Num.26.1|Numbers 26:1]],[[Num.26.3|3]],[[Num.26.63|63]]\) and the allocation of lands to the tribes \([[Num.32.2|Numbers 32:2]],[[Num.32.28|28]]; [[Num.34.17|34:17]]; [[Josh.14.1|Joshua 14:1]]; [[Josh.17.4|17:4]]; [[Josh.19.51|19:51]]\). Eleazar succeeded Aaron as high priest \([[Num.20.25|Numbers 20:25-28]]; [[Deut.10.6|Deuteronomy 10:6]]\) and served in that role during the conquest of Canaan \([[Josh.21.1|Joshua 21:1]]; [[Josh.22.13|22:13]],[[Josh.22.31|31]],[[Josh.22.32|32]]\). He played a key role in the distribution of the Levitical cities \([[Josh.21.1|Joshua 21:1]]\) and died during the time of Joshua \([[Josh.24.33|Joshua 24:33]]\). His descendants continued to serve as priests \([[Judg.20.28|Judges 20:28]]; [[1Chr.6.3|1 Chronicles 6:3-4]],[[1Chr.6.50|50]]; [[1Chr.9.20|9:20]]; [[1Chr.24.1|24:1-6]]; [[Ezra.7.5|Ezra 7:5]]\).
