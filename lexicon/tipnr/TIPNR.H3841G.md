<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Libnah</span>, a place where the Israelites camped during their wilderness journey \([[Num.33.20|Numbers 33:20-21]]\).
- A location near Ashnah **<big>[[H0823|אַשְׁנָה]]</big>** first mentioned at [[Num.33.20|Num.33.20]]; 
- only referred to as Libnah **<big>[[H3841|לִבְנָה]]</big>**.<br>

Libnah is mentioned in [[Num.33.20|Numbers 33:20-21]] as one of the places where the Israelites camped during their journey through the wilderness after leaving Egypt. The Israelites moved from Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah. Then they set out from Libnah and camped at Rissah.
