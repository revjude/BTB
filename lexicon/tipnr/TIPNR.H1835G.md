Dan was one of the twelve tribes of Israel, descended from Jacob's fifth son, Dan \([[Gen.30.6|Genesis 30:6]]\). The tribe was allotted territory in the northern part of Canaan \([[Josh.19.40|Joshua 19:40-48]]\). The city of Dan, originally known as Laish or Leshem, was conquered by the Danites \([[Josh.19.47|Joshua 19:47]]; [[Judg.18.29|Judges 18:29]]\). It became the northernmost city of Israel, often used in the phrase "from Dan to Beersheba" to describe the entire land \([[Judg.20.1|Judges 20:1]]; [[1Sam.3.20|1 Samuel 3:20]]\). Jeroboam set up one of his golden calves in Dan \([[1Kgs.12.29|1 Kings 12:29-30]]\), leading to idolatry. The city was also known for its skilled craftsmen \([[2Chr.2.14|2 Chronicles 2:14]]\). Dan was one of the first places to fall to foreign invaders due to its northern location \([[Jer.4.15|Jeremiah 4:15]]; [[Jer.8.16|8:16]]\).
