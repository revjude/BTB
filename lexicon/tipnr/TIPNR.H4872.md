Moses was born to Amram and Jochebed, Levites in Egypt, at a time when Pharaoh had commanded that all Hebrew male infants be killed \([[Exod.2.1|Exodus 2:1-2]]\). His mother hid him for three months, then placed him in a basket on the Nile where Pharaoh's daughter found and adopted him \([[Exod.2.3|Exodus 2:3-10]]\). As an adult, Moses killed an Egyptian who was beating a Hebrew and fled to Midian \([[Exod.2.11|Exodus 2:11-15]]\). There he married Zipporah and worked as a shepherd for his father-in-law Jethro \([[Exod.2.16|Exodus 2:16-22]]\). 

God appeared to Moses in a burning bush and called him to lead Israel out of Egypt \([[Exod.3|Exodus 3-4]]\). Moses, along with his brother Aaron, went to Pharaoh demanding that he let Israel go \([[Exod.5.1|Exodus 5:1]]\). Through a series of ten plagues, God compelled Pharaoh to release the Israelites \([[Exod.7|Exodus 7-12]]\). Moses led them out of Egypt, through the Red Sea, and to Mount Sinai \([[Exod.13|Exodus 13-19]]\). There God gave Moses the law and instructions for the tabernacle \([[Exod.20|Exodus 20-31]]\). 

While Moses was on the mountain, Israel made and worshiped a golden calf \([[Exod.32|Exodus 32]]\). Moses interceded for the people and God relented from destroying them \([[Exod.32|Exodus 32-34]]\). Moses oversaw the construction of the tabernacle \([[Exod.35|Exodus 35-40]]\). He led Israel for forty years in the wilderness, mediating between them and God \(Numbers, Deuteronomy\). God prohibited Moses from entering Canaan because of his disobedience at Meribah \([[Num.20.1|Numbers 20:1-13]]\). Before his death, Moses viewed the promised land from Mount Nebo \([[Deut.34.1|Deuteronomy 34:1-4]]\). He died at 120 years old and was buried by God \([[Deut.34.5|Deuteronomy 34:5-8]]\). 

The New Testament refers to Moses as the giver of the law \([[John.1.17|John 1:17]]\), a type of Christ \([[John.3.14|John 3:14]]\), and the greatest prophet of the old covenant \([[Acts.3.22|Acts 3:22-23]]\).
