Israel, also known as Jacob, was the son of Isaac and Rebekah, and the grandson of Abraham. He was born after his twin brother Esau \([[Gen.25.24|Gen. 25:24-26]]\). 

Jacob bought Esau's birthright for a bowl of stew when Esau was famished \([[Gen.25.29|Gen. 25:29-34]]\). He then deceived his father Isaac into giving him the firstborn blessing \([[Gen.27|Gen. 27]]\). To escape Esau's anger, Jacob fled to his uncle Laban in Paddan-aram \([[Gen.28.1|Gen. 28:1-5]]\). 

On the way, Jacob had a dream of a stairway to heaven at Bethel, where God confirmed the covenant promises to him \([[Gen.28.10|Gen. 28:10-22]]\). Jacob worked for Laban for 14 years, marrying his daughters Leah and Rachel \([[Gen.29|Gen. 29]]\). 

Wanting to return home, Jacob left Paddan-aram with his family and flocks. On the way, he wrestled with God at Peniel and was renamed Israel, meaning "he struggles with God" \([[Gen.32.22|Gen. 32:22-32]]\). Israel \(or 'Jacob'\) reconciled with Esau and settled in Canaan \([[Gen.33|Gen. 33]]\). 

Jacob had twelve sons who became the ancestors of the twelve tribes of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Joseph, Benjamin, Dan, Naphtali, Gad and Asher \([[Gen.35.23|Gen. 35:23-26]]\). 

Jacob showed favoritism to Joseph, provoking his brothers to sell Joseph into slavery in Egypt \([[Gen.37|Gen. 37]]\). Later, during a famine, Jacob's sons went to Egypt for food and were reconciled with Joseph, who had risen to power there \([[Gen.42|Gen. 42-46]]\). 

Jacob moved his whole family to Egypt, where he lived for 17 years \([[Gen.47.28|Gen. 47:28]]\). Before his death, Jacob blessed Joseph's sons Ephraim and Manasseh \([[Gen.48|Gen. 48]]\) and gave prophetic blessings to his own twelve sons \([[Gen.49|Gen. 49]]\). He died at age 147 and was buried in Canaan \([[Gen.49.33|Gen. 49:33]]; [[Gen.50.1|50:1-14]]\). 

God often identified Himself as the "God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob" and reaffirmed His covenant promises to Jacob's descendants \(e.g. [[Exod.3.6|Exo. 3:6]],[[Exod.3.15|15]]\). The nation of Israel took its name from Jacob and the Jewish people are his descendants. The name Jeshurun is used as a poetic name for Israel \([[Deut.32.15|Deut. 32:15]]; [[Deut.33.5|33:5]],[[Deut.33.26|26]]; [[Isa.44.2|Isa. 44:2]]\).
