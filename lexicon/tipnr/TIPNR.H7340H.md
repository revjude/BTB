<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Rehob</span> was a Levitical city within the territory of Asher \([[Josh.19.30|Jos. 19:30]]; [[Josh.21.31|21:31]]; [[1Chr.6.75|1 Ch. 6:75]]\).
- A location near Helkath **<big>[[H2520|חֶלְקַת]]</big>** first mentioned at [[Josh.19.30|Jos.19.30]]; 
- only referred to as Rehob **<big>[[H7340|רְחֹב]]</big>**.<br>

Rehob was a city allotted to the tribe of Asher \([[Josh.19.30|Jos. 19:30]]\) and designated as a Levitical city, given to the descendants of Gershon \([[Josh.21.31|Jos. 21:31]]; [[1Chr.6.75|1 Ch. 6:75]]\). However, the people of Asher did not drive out the Canaanite inhabitants of Rehob, so they continued to live among them \([[Judg.1.31|Jdg. 1:31]]\). The exact location of this city is uncertain.
