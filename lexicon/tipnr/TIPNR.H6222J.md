<span class='tipnrhighlight'>Asaiah</span>, a descendant of Judah, lived in Jerusalem \([[1Chr.9.5|1 Chr 9:5]]\).
- A man living at the time of Exile and Return, only mentioned at [[1Chr.9.5|1Ch.9.5]]; 
- only referred to as Asaiah **<big>[[H6222|עֲשָׂיָה]]</big>**.

Asaiah was a descendant of Judah mentioned in a list of people who lived in Jerusalem, recorded in [[1Chr.9|1 Chronicles 9]]. He is described as "the firstborn of the Shilonites," which likely refers to a branch of the tribe of Judah. The context suggests that this list represents those who resettled in Jerusalem after the Babylonian exile. As a descendant of Judah living in Jerusalem, Asaiah was part of the group that helped repopulate the city and restore its community.
