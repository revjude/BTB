Elkanah, an Ephraimite from Ramathaim-zophim, was the father of the prophet Samuel and the husband of Hannah and Peninnah \([[1Sam.1.1|1Sa.1.1]],[[1Sam.1.4|4]]\). He was a devout man who regularly went to worship and sacrifice to the Lord at Shiloh \([[1Sam.1.3|1Sa.1.3]]\). Elkanah had two wives, Hannah and Peninnah. While Peninnah had children, Hannah was barren \([[1Sam.1.2|1Sa.1.2]]\). Despite Hannah's inability to bear children, Elkanah loved her deeply and tried to comfort her \([[1Sam.1.8|1Sa.1.8]]\). Hannah prayed fervently for a child and vowed to dedicate him to the Lord's service \([[1Sam.1.11|1Sa.1.11]]\). After the birth of Samuel, Elkanah supported Hannah in fulfilling her vow and brought the child to serve in the tabernacle under Eli the priest \([[1Sam.1.21|1Sa.1.21-28]]\). Elkanah continued to worship at Shiloh and received a blessing from Eli for his devotion \([[1Sam.2.20|1Sa.2.20]]\). He is also mentioned in the genealogies of the Levites as an ancestor of Samuel and his sons \([[1Chr.6.27|1Ch.6.27]],[[1Chr.6.34|34]]\). Elkanah's story highlights his faithfulness, love for his family, and support for Hannah's dedication of Samuel to the Lord's service.
