Esau, also known as Edom, was the firstborn son of Isaac and Rebekah and the twin brother of Israel \(or 'Jacob'\) \([[Gen.25.25|Genesis 25:25-26]]\). He was a skillful hunter and his father's favorite son \([[Gen.25.27|Genesis 25:27-28]]\). Esau sold his birthright to Jacob for a bowl of stew when he was famished \([[Gen.25.29|Genesis 25:29-34]]\). He married two Hittite women, causing grief to his parents \([[Gen.26.34|Genesis 26:34-35]]\). Esau lost his father's blessing to Jacob through deception \([[Gen.27|Genesis 27]]\). He held a grudge against Jacob but later reconciled with him \([[Gen.32|Genesis 32-33]]\). Esau settled in the hill country of Seir, which became known as Edom \([[Gen.36.8|Genesis 36:8-9]]\). He had five sons and became the father of the Edomites \([[Gen.36.1|Genesis 36:1-43]]\). The Edomites were later considered enemies of Israel \([[Num.20.14|Numbers 20:14-21]]; [[1Sam.14.47|1 Samuel 14:47]]; [[2Sam.8.13|2 Samuel 8:13-14]]\). God's love for Jacob and His rejection of Esau is used as an example of His sovereignty \([[Mal.1.2|Malachi 1:2-3]]; [[Rom.9.13|Romans 9:13]]\).
