*The Collection for the Saints*

[[1Cor.16.1|1]] Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I have given order to the churches of Galatia, even so do ye.

1: 

[[1Cor.16.2|2]] Upon the first \[day\] of the week let every one of you lay by him in store, as \[God\] hath prospered him, that there be no gatherings when I come.

2: 

[[1Cor.16.3|3]] And when I come, whomsoever ye shall approve by \[your\] letters, them will I send to bring your liberality unto Jerusalem.

3: 

[[1Cor.16.4|4]] And if it be meet that I go also, they shall go with me.
