*Dedication of the Temple*

[[Ezra.6.16|16]] And the children of Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity, kept the dedication of this house of God with joy,

16: 

[[Ezra.6.17|17]] And offered at the dedication of this house of God an hundred bullocks, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs; and for a sin offering for all Israel, twelve he goats, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

17: 

[[Ezra.6.18|18]] And they set the priests in their divisions, and the Levites in their courses, for the service of God, which \[is\] at Jerusalem; as it is written in the book of Moses.
