*Jesus Heals at Gennesaret*

[[Mark.6.53|53]] And when they had passed over, they came into the land of Gennesaret, and drew to the shore.

53: 

[[Mark.6.54|54]] And when they were come out of the ship, straightway they knew him,

54: 

[[Mark.6.55|55]] And ran through that whole region round about, and began to carry about in beds those that were sick, where they heard he was.

55:
