*The Soldiers Mock Jesus*

[[Mark.15.16|16]] And the soldiers led him away into the hall, called Praetorium; and they call together the whole band.

16: 

[[Mark.15.17|17]] And they clothed him with purple, and platted a crown of thorns, and put it about his \[head\],

17: 

[[Mark.15.18|18]] And began to salute him, Hail, King of the Jews!

18: 

[[Mark.15.19|19]] And they smote him on the head with a reed, and did spit upon him, and bowing \[their\] knees worshipped him.

19: 

[[Mark.15.20|20]] And when they had mocked him, they took off the purple from him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him out to crucify him.
