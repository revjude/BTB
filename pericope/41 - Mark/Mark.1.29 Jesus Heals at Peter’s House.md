*Jesus Heals at Peter’s House*

[[Mark.1.29|29]] And forthwith, when they were come out of the synagogue, they entered into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.

29: 

[[Mark.1.30|30]] But Simon’s wife’s mother lay sick of a fever, and anon they tell him of her.

30: 

[[Mark.1.31|31]] And he came and took her by the hand, and lifted her up; and immediately the fever left her, and she ministered unto them.

31: 

[[Mark.1.32|32]] And at even, when the sun did set, they brought unto him all that were diseased, and them that were possessed with devils.

32: 

[[Mark.1.33|33]] And all the city was gathered together at the door.

33: 

[[Mark.1.34|34]] And he healed many that were sick of divers diseases, and cast out many devils; and suffered not the devils to speak, because they knew him.
