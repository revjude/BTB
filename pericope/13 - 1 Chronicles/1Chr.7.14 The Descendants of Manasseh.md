*The Descendants of Manasseh*

[[1Chr.7.14|14]] The sons of Manasseh; Ashriel, whom she bare: (\[but\] his concubine the Aramitess bare Machir the father of Gilead:

14: 

[[1Chr.7.15|15]] And Machir took to wife \[the sister\] of Huppim and Shuppim, whose sister’s name \[was\] Maachah;) and the name of the second \[was\] Zelophehad: and Zelophehad had daughters.

15: 

[[1Chr.7.16|16]] And Maachah the wife of Machir bare a son, and she called his name Peresh; and the name of his brother \[was\] Sheresh; and his sons \[were\] Ulam and Rakem.

16: 

[[1Chr.7.17|17]] And the sons of Ulam; Bedan. These \[were\] the sons of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh.

17: 

[[1Chr.7.18|18]] And his sister Hammoleketh bare Ishod, and Abiezer, and Mahalah.

18:
