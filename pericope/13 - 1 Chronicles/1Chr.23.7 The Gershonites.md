*The Gershonites*

[[1Chr.23.7|7]] Of the Gershonites \[were\], Laadan, and Shimei.

7: 

[[1Chr.23.8|8]] The sons of Laadan; the chief \[was\] Jehiel, and Zetham, and Joel, three.

8: 

[[1Chr.23.9|9]] The sons of Shimei; Shelomith, and Haziel, and Haran, three. These \[were\] the chief of the fathers of Laadan.

9: 

[[1Chr.23.10|10]] And the sons of Shimei \[were\], Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah. These four \[were\] the sons of Shimei.

10: 

[[1Chr.23.11|11]] And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second: but Jeush and Beriah had not many sons; therefore they were in one reckoning, according to \[their\] father’s house.
