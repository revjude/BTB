*The Descendants of Abraham*

[[1Chr.1.28|28]] The sons of Abraham; Isaac, and Ishmael.

28: 

[[1Chr.1.29|29]] These \[are\] their generations: The firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,

29: 

[[1Chr.1.30|30]] Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema,

30: 

[[1Chr.1.31|31]] Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael.

31: 

[[1Chr.1.32|32]] Now the sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan; Sheba, and Dedan.

32: 

[[1Chr.1.33|33]] And the sons of Midian; Ephah, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaah. All these \[are\] the sons of Keturah.

33: 

[[1Chr.1.34|34]] And Abraham begat Isaac. The sons of Isaac; Esau and Israel.
