*The Descendants of Issachar*

[[1Chr.7.1|1]] Now the sons of Issachar \[were\], Tola, and Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, four.

1: 

[[1Chr.7.2|2]] And the sons of Tola; Uzzi, and Rephaiah, and Jeriel, and Jahmai, and Jibsam, and Shemuel, heads of their father’s house, \[to wit\], of Tola: \[they were\] valiant men of might in their generations; whose number \[was\] in the days of David two and twenty thousand and six hundred.

2: 

[[1Chr.7.3|3]] And the sons of Uzzi; Izrahiah: and the sons of Izrahiah; Michael, and Obadiah, and Joel, Ishiah, five: all of them chief men.

3: 

[[1Chr.7.4|4]] And with them, by their generations, after the house of their fathers, \[were\] bands of soldiers for war, six and thirty thousand \[men\]: for they had many wives and sons.

4: 

[[1Chr.7.5|5]] And their brethren among all the families of Issachar \[were\] valiant men of might, reckoned in all by their genealogies fourscore and seven thousand.
