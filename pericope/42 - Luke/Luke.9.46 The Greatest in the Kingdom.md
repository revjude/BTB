*The Greatest in the Kingdom*

[[Luke.9.46|46]] Then there arose a reasoning among them, which of them should be greatest.

46: 

[[Luke.9.47|47]] And Jesus, perceiving the thought of their heart, took a child, and set him by him,

47: 

[[Luke.9.48|48]] And said unto them, Whosoever shall receive this child in my name receiveth me: and whosoever shall receive me receiveth him that sent me: for he that is least among you all, the same shall be great.

48: 

[[Luke.9.49|49]] And John answered and said, Master, we saw one casting out devils in thy name; and we forbad him, because he followeth not with us.

49: 

[[Luke.9.50|50]] And Jesus said unto him, Forbid \[him\] not: for he that is not against us is for us.
