*A Call to Repentance*

[[Luke.13.1|1]] There were present at that season some that told him of the Galilaeans, whose blood Pilate had mingled with their sacrifices.

1: 

[[Luke.13.2|2]] And Jesus answering said unto them, Suppose ye that these Galilaeans were sinners above all the Galilaeans, because they suffered such things?

2: 

[[Luke.13.3|3]] I tell you, Nay: but, except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish.

3: 

[[Luke.13.4|4]] Or those eighteen, upon whom the tower in Siloam fell, and slew them, think ye that they were sinners above all men that dwelt in Jerusalem?

4:
