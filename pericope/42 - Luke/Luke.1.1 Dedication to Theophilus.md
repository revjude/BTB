<small>[[Luke.1|Luke 1:1-4]]</small>
*Dedication to Theophilus*

[[Luke.1.1|1]] Forasmuch as many have taken in hand to set forth in order a declaration of those things which are most surely believed among us,

1: 

[[Luke.1.2|2]] Even as they delivered them unto us, which from the beginning were eyewitnesses, and ministers of the word;

2: 

[[Luke.1.3|3]] It seemed good to me also, having had perfect understanding of all things from the very first, to write unto thee in order, most excellent Theophilus,

3: 

[[Luke.1.4|4]] That thou mightest know the certainty of those things, wherein thou hast been instructed.

4:
