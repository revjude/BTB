*The Death of Abraham*

[[Gen.25.7|7]] And these \[are\] the days of the years of Abraham’s life which he lived, an hundred threescore and fifteen years.

7: 

[[Gen.25.8|8]] Then Abraham gave up the ghost, and died in a good old age, an old man, and full \[of years\]; and was gathered to his people.

8: 

[[Gen.25.9|9]] And his sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron the son of Zohar the Hittite, which \[is\] before Mamre;

9: 

[[Gen.25.10|10]] The field which Abraham purchased of the sons of Heth: there was Abraham buried, and Sarah his wife.

10: 

[[Gen.25.11|11]] And it came to pass after the death of Abraham, that God blessed his son Isaac; and Isaac dwelt by the well Lahairoi.
