[[Gen.46.10|10]] And the sons of Simeon; Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanitish woman.

[[Gen.46.11|11]] And the sons of Levi; Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

11:

[[Gen.46.14|14]] And the sons of Zebulun; Sered, and Elon, and Jahleel.

14:
