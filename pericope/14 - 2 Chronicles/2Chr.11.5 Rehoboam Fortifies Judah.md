*Rehoboam Fortifies Judah*

[[2Chr.11.5|5]] And Rehoboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and built cities for defence in Judah.

5: 

[[2Chr.11.6|6]] He built even Bethlehem, and Etam, and Tekoa,

6: 

[[2Chr.11.7|7]] And Bethzur, and Shoco, and Adullam,

7: 

[[2Chr.11.8|8]] And Gath, and Mareshah, and Ziph,

8: 

[[2Chr.11.9|9]] And Adoraim, and Lachish, and Azekah,

9: 

[[2Chr.11.10|10]] And Zorah, and Aijalon, and Hebron, which \[are\] in Judah and in Benjamin fenced cities.

10: 

[[2Chr.11.11|11]] And he fortified the strong holds, and put captains in them, and store of victual, and of oil and wine.

11: 

[[2Chr.11.12|12]] And in every several city \[he put\] shields and spears, and made them exceeding strong, having Judah and Benjamin on his side.
