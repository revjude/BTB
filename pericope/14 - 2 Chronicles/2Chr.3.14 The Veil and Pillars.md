*The Veil and Pillars*

[[2Chr.3.14|14]] And he made the vail \[of\] blue, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen, and wrought cherubims thereon.

14: 

[[2Chr.3.15|15]] Also he made before the house two pillars of thirty and five cubits high, and the chapiter that \[was\] on the top of each of them \[was\] five cubits.

15: 

[[2Chr.3.16|16]] And he made chains, \[as\] in the oracle, and put \[them\] on the heads of the pillars; and made an hundred pomegranates, and put \[them\] on the chains.

16: 

[[2Chr.3.17|17]] And he reared up the pillars before the temple, one on the right hand, and the other on the left; and called the name of that on the right hand Jachin, and the name of that on the left Boaz.
