*Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread*

[[Lev.23.4|4]] These \[are\] the feasts of the LORD, \[even\] holy convocations, which ye shall proclaim in their seasons.

4: 

[[Lev.23.5|5]] In the fourteenth \[day\] of the first month at even \[is\] the LORD’S passover.

5: 

[[Lev.23.6|6]] And on the fifteenth day of the same month \[is\] the feast of unleavened bread unto the LORD: seven days ye must eat unleavened bread.

6: 

[[Lev.23.7|7]] In the first day ye shall have an holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work therein.

7: 

[[Lev.23.8|8]] But ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD seven days: in the seventh day \[is\] an holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work \[therein\].
