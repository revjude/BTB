*God’s Testimony about His Son*

[[1John.5.9|9]] If we receive the witness of men, the witness of God is greater: for this is the witness of God which he hath testified of his Son.

9: 

[[1John.5.10|10]] He that believeth on the Son of God hath the witness in himself: he that believeth not God hath made him a liar; because he believeth not the record that God gave of his Son.
