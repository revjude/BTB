*Offerings by the Exiles*

70: 

[[Neh.7.71|71]] And \[some\] of the chief of the fathers gave to the treasure of the work twenty thousand drams of gold, and two thousand and two hundred pound of silver.

71: 

[[Neh.7.72|72]] And \[that\] which the rest of the people gave \[was\] twenty thousand drams of gold, and two thousand pound of silver, and threescore and seven priests’ garments.

72: 

[[Neh.7.73|73]] So the priests, and the Levites, and the porters, and the singers, and \[some\] of the people, and the Nethinims, and all Israel, dwelt in their cities; and when the seventh month came, the children of Israel \[were\] in their cities.
