*Jesus Predicts Peter’s Denial*

[[John.13.36|36]] Simon Peter said unto him, Lord, whither goest thou? Jesus answered him, Whither I go, thou canst not follow me now; but thou shalt follow me afterwards.

36: 

[[John.13.37|37]] Peter said unto him, Lord, why cannot I follow thee now? I will lay down my life for thy sake.

37: 

[[John.13.38|38]] Jesus answered him, Wilt thou lay down thy life for my sake? Verily, verily, I say unto thee, The cock shall not crow, till thou hast denied me thrice.
