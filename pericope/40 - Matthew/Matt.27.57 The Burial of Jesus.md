<small>[[Matt.27|Matthew 27:57-61]]</small>
*The Burial of Jesus*

[[Matt.27.57|57]] When the even was come, there came a rich man of Arimathaea, named Joseph, who also himself was Jesus’ disciple:

57: 

[[Matt.27.58|58]] He went to Pilate, and begged the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be delivered.

58: 

[[Matt.27.59|59]] And when Joseph had taken the body, he wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,

59: 

[[Matt.27.60|60]] And laid it in his own new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock: and he rolled a great stone to the door of the sepulchre, and departed.

60: 

[[Matt.27.61|61]] And there was Mary Magdalene, and the other Mary, sitting over against the sepulchre.
