*The Leper’s Prayer*

[[Matt.8.1|1]] When he was come down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him.

1: 

[[Matt.8.2|2]] And, behold, there came a leper and worshipped him, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.

3:
