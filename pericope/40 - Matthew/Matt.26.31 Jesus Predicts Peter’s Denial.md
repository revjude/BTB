*Jesus Predicts Peter’s Denial*

[[Matt.26.31|31]] Then saith Jesus unto them, All ye shall be offended because of me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be scattered abroad.

31: 

[[Matt.26.32|32]] But after I am risen again, I will go before you into Galilee.

32: 

[[Matt.26.33|33]] Peter answered and said unto him, Though all \[men\] shall be offended because of thee, \[yet\] will I never be offended.

33: 

[[Matt.26.34|34]] Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, That this night, before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.

34: 

[[Matt.26.35|35]] Peter said unto him, Though I should die with thee, yet will I not deny thee. Likewise also said all the disciples.
