<small>[[Matt.5|Matthew 5:3-12]]</small>
*The Beatitudes*

[[Matt.5.3|3]] Blessed \[are\] the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

3: 

[[Matt.5.4|4]] Blessed \[are\] they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.

4: 

[[Matt.5.5|5]] Blessed \[are\] the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.

5: 

[[Matt.5.6|6]] Blessed \[are\] they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled.

6: 

[[Matt.5.7|7]] Blessed \[are\] the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.

7: 

[[Matt.5.8|8]] Blessed \[are\] the pure in heart: for they shall see God.

8: 

[[Matt.5.9|9]] Blessed \[are\] the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.

9: 

[[Matt.5.10|10]] Blessed \[are\] they which are persecuted for righteousness’ sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

10: 

[[Matt.5.11|11]] Blessed are ye, when \[men\] shall revile you, and persecute \[you\], and shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake.

11: 

[[Matt.5.12|12]] Rejoice, and be exceeding glad: for great \[is\] your reward in heaven: for so persecuted they the prophets which were before you.
