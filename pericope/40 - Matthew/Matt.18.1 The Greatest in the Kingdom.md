*The Greatest in the Kingdom*

[[Matt.18.1|1]] At the same time came the disciples unto Jesus, saying, Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?

1: 

[[Matt.18.2|2]] And Jesus called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them,

2: 

[[Matt.18.3|3]] And said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.

3: 

[[Matt.18.4|4]] Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven.

4: 

[[Matt.18.5|5]] And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me.
