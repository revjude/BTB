*Deliver Me, O LORD!*

[[Ps.3.0|0]] A Psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom his son.

0: 

[[Ps.3.1|1]] LORD, how are they increased that trouble me! many \[are\] they that rise up against me.

1: 

[[Ps.3.2|2]] Many \[there be\] which say of my soul, \[There is\] no help for him in God. Selah.

2: 

[[Ps.3.3|3]] But thou, O LORD, \[art\] a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.

3: 

[[Ps.3.4|4]] I cried unto the LORD with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill. Selah.

4: 

[[Ps.3.5|5]] I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the LORD sustained me.

5: 

[[Ps.3.6|6]] I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have set \[themselves\] against me round about.

6: 

[[Ps.3.7|7]] Arise, O LORD; save me, O my God: for thou hast smitten all mine enemies \[upon\] the cheek bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly.

7: 

[[Ps.3.8|8]] Salvation \[belongeth\] unto the LORD: thy blessing \[is\] upon thy people. Selah.
