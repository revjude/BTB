<small>[[Acts.15|Acts 15:1-4]]</small>
*The Dispute over Circumcision*

[[Acts.15.1|1]] And certain men which came down from Judaea taught the brethren, \[and said\], Except ye be circumcised after the manner of Moses, ye cannot be saved.

1: 

[[Acts.15.2|2]] When therefore Paul and Barnabas had no small dissension and disputation with them, they determined that Paul and Barnabas, and certain other of them, should go up to Jerusalem unto the apostles and elders about this question.

2: 

[[Acts.15.3|3]] And being brought on their way by the church, they passed through Phenice and Samaria, declaring the conversion of the Gentiles: and they caused great joy unto all the brethren.

3: 

[[Acts.15.4|4]] And when they were come to Jerusalem, they were received of the church, and \[of\] the apostles and elders, and they declared all things that God had done with them.
