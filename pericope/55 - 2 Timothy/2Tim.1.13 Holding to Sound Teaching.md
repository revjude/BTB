*Holding to Sound Teaching*

[[2Tim.1.13|13]] Hold fast the form of sound words, which thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.

13: 

[[2Tim.1.14|14]] That good thing which was committed unto thee keep by the Holy Ghost which dwelleth in us.

14: 

[[2Tim.1.15|15]] This thou knowest, that all they which are in Asia be turned away from me; of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.

15:

17:
