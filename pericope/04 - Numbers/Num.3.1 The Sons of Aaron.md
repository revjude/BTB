*The Sons of Aaron*

[[Num.3.1|1]] These also \[are\] the generations of Aaron and Moses in the day \[that\] the LORD spake with Moses in mount Sinai.

1: 

[[Num.3.2|2]] And these \[are\] the names of the sons of Aaron; Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

2:
